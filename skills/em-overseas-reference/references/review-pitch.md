# Review and submission packaging

## Review dimensions

Inspect the audience promise, protagonist agency, relationship movement, causal/world rules, pacing and payoff, emotion, cultural fit, format and production needs, package completeness, and the current recipient's requirements.

Record **severity → source location/short evidence → effect on the requested result → smallest useful fix → protected elements**. A suggestion is not permission to rewrite.

Use three statuses: a material issue blocking the next dependent deliverable; advice that does not block delivery; and an unverified claim or requirement. A conflict may block a particular edit without blocking an analysis report.

## Six package components

1. Project overview and positioning.
2. A clear logline and principal characters.
3. A complete synopsis including the ending, unless the recipient explicitly requests a different form.
4. Existing episode outline within the requested range.
5. Sample scenes or episodes from the authorized material.
6. Production, rights, and provenance information available from the user, with gaps labeled.

Do not invent an ending, chain of title, budget, completed sample, or platform interest to fill a form.

## Verify the actual recipient

| Field | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Entity | The real company/platform and the relevant receiving team |
| Territory and language | Country, intended audience, delivery language |
| Product | Script submission, finished video, or another deliverable |
| Production mode | Live action, animation, AI, or a stated combination |
| Open channel | A currently verified submission route and its date |
| Format | Required components, length, file type, and review stage |
| Rights and compensation | Actual supplied terms; mark unknowns rather than guessing |

G06's platform list is a historical set of research leads, not verification of ownership, policy, rates, or open submissions. It supplies no currently verified email route. Historical claims such as “original projects up to 100,000” or “adaptations from 30,000” lack a reliable current currency/date/contract context and must not be quoted as offers.

Prepare the package requested. Do not automatically upload it, email a platform, contact a person, or promise acceptance.

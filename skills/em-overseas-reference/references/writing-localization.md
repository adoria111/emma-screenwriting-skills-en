# Drafting, format, and localization

## From outline to scene

Turn the authorized outline into goals, actions, obstacles, choices, reactions, and a changed state. Dialogue should do a job within that action. Preserve the locked relationship, information order, clues, and ending. Check every added detail against the authorized scope.

When no format is specified, the pack's working-script example uses an episode/scene heading, time, interior/exterior, location, and a separate Characters line before action. The English equivalent below demonstrates format only:

```text
1-1 INT. WATCH SHOP - DAY
Characters: Mara, Eli; caller (phone)

△ Mara places the key on the counter. Eli leaves it there.
MARA: I came for the ledger, too.
```

List people actually visible or audible, including functional roles. Mark a phone voice, a photo appearance, or other special mode accurately. People only mentioned do not belong in the participant line; use “None” if no one appears or speaks. Merge consecutive speech from the same person unless a meaningful action, interruption, or sound-mode change requires separation.

Chinese working-script conventions and English screenplay specifications are different formats. For an English submission, use INT./EXT., present-tense action, and the recipient's requirements. Do not automatically translate Chinese internal “OS” into English screenplay O.S.; distinguish internal voice, voice-over, and someone speaking off screen.

## Localize the dramatic function

For each proposed substitution record:

| Source function | Target decision |
| --- | --- |
| Motivation and pressure | What makes this character choose the same consequential action? |
| Authority and power | Who can actually enforce the obstacle in this setting? |
| Evidence | What object, record, or witness can establish the fact? |
| Emotional meaning | What must the audience understand or feel? |
| Candidate substitution | What changes, and what follows from it? |
| Authorization and verification | Is the change allowed, supported, and chosen? |

Do not assume every American character is white or every setting is California. Region, ethnicity, class, religion, and profession can matter; follow the project. Initial Chinese-to-US adaptation belongs to `em-us-localization`; polishing an established English version belongs to `em-english-polish`.

## Production fit

Identify actual cost drivers in live action: locations, crowds, stunts, children, animals, weather, period requirements, and effects. For AI work, use current tests and the actual handoff rather than permanent capability claims. Simplify execution only with equivalent meaning; do not delete a necessary clue, witness, or causal action just to make a shot easier.

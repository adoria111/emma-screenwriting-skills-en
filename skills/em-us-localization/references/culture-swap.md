## Places

Shanghai → Los Angeles; Beijing → New York/Washington, DC; Shenzhen → San Francisco/Silicon Valley are historical candidate mappings, not equivalents or automatic swaps. Choose actual geography by industry, distance, culture, and story function.

Suburbs → suburbs; residential compound → gated community/neighborhood; office building → office tower/corporate building; hotel lobby → hotel lobby; nightclub/KTV → club/lounge if performance/privacy functions match; wet market → grocery store/farmers market if interaction functions match.

## Vehicles/luxury

BMW, Mercedes, Porsche, Range Rover, Tesla, Hermès, and Louis Vuitton can retain their established brand names where appropriate. Brands alone do not establish US authenticity or require product placement.

## Workplace/business

Board chair → chairman/chair/executive chairman; company head → CEO/president; department head → director; manager → manager; assistant → assistant; S-tier project → S-rated/flagship project if the organization uses that category; listed company → publicly traded company; falling share price → stock dropped/shares fell. Titles must match actual authority.

## Family language

“Blood is thicker than water” is already an English idiom; “Family is family” may fit another register. Retirement funds may mean savings or pension, with different ownership/access. Criticism of an elder may be rendered through behavior rather than a literal honorific insult. “Still single” preserves status without automatically endorsing age stigma. Translate filial duty through the character's intended behavior/pressure. For an “ungrateful child” or demeaning label for a daughter, preserve the speaker's contempt in context rather than literal imagery.

## Time notation

03:00 → 3:00 AM; 09:00 → 9:00 AM; 14:17 → 2:17 PM; 23:00 → 11:00 PM. Follow the actual project format; spoken dialogue may use natural conversational time.

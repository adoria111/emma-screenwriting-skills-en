## Principles

1. Surnames follow actual marriage, adoption, and kinship. A shared family surname is common, not mandatory.
2. US localization is not whitening. Preserve approved ethnicity, period, and background. English first names do not establish ethnicity.
3. Fit period/person. Traditional names may be appropriate; the bank does not invalidate established characters.
4. Carter can fit different professional roles, but avoid confusing duplicates within one project.
5. Favor readable, distinct names; avoid confusing sound-alikes. Approved names outrank the bank.
